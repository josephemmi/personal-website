About

Hi, I'm Joseph.

Product Manager, based in Glasgow, Scotland.

I'm a Product Manager, passionate about human behaviour and the intersection between technology, business and design to create great products and services.

Most of my career has been spent inside large organisations — Scottish Water and Weir Group among them — working on everything from customer self-service platforms to early experiments with voice interfaces. Alongside that, I spent several years as a founder, building and leading Agatha, a safety app for solo runners and cyclists, from idea through to a shipped product.

I believe in products and services that really work — built to make life better and easier for the people using them, not just easier to sell.

Outside of product work, I write, take photographs, and build small things on the side — currently Momentini, a baby tracker I built for my own use with Claude Code.
