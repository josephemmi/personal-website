I'm a product builder, designer and founder based in Glasgow, currently leading product for Weir's Digital Ecosystem. I've spent the last decade moving between consulting, startups, public sector transformation and enterprise product leadership — and I've come to care more about the thread that connects them than about any one label.

## Venezuela, Spain, Scotland

I was born in Venezuela, spent part of my childhood in Spain, and have lived in Scotland for most of my adult life. Moving between countries as a child teaches you to arrive somewhere unfamiliar, work out quickly what actually matters, and rebuild a sense of normal from scratch — a skill that turned out to be surprisingly useful in product work, where every new team and every new problem asks for the same thing.

## Product journey

My path into product wasn't a straight line. I started in SAP consulting, learning how large organisations actually make decisions — slowly, and rarely for the reasons the org chart suggests. From there I moved into a small design studio, then co-founded a startup, then spent years leading product across public sector transformation, financial services and now industrial technology at Weir. Each stop taught me something the others couldn't: consulting taught diagnosis, the studio taught craft, and enterprise work taught patience with systems bigger than any one team.

## Founder journey

Co-founding Agatha remains the sharpest learning experience of my career. Nothing teaches you what actually matters in product like being the person accountable for whether the company survives the quarter. It changed how I think about speed, evidence and scope — lessons that have shaped every product role I've held since.

## Why this site exists

This isn't a CV, even though a CV's worth of experience sits behind it. It's closer to a running record of the things I'm building, writing and photographing — because the honest answer to "what do you do" has never fit neatly into a job title.
