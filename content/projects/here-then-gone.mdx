Here Then Gone is a long-running photography project about impermanence — buildings before demolition, shopfronts before closure, light before it changes, moments before they pass.

It started without a plan, as a habit of photographing things I suspected wouldn't be there much longer. Over time it became a more deliberate practice: a way of paying attention to change that usually happens too slowly, or too quietly, to notice until it's already happened.

The project sits at the intersection of documentary photography and personal reflection — less about any single striking image, and more about the accumulated record of things that used to be here.

## Approach

- Shot primarily on location in Glasgow and during travel
- No fixed schedule — the project runs on attention, not a shot list
- Photographs are paired with short notes on what's changing and why

Ongoing, with new work added as it happens. See the [photography collection](/photography/here-then-gone) for a selection of images.
