Attimino — a small moment — is the smallest project here by design: one short, specific observation a day, written down instead of let go.

It's a deliberate counterweight to a working life spent thinking in roadmaps, quarters and outcomes. Attimino has no goal, no roadmap and no outcome beyond the practice itself: noticing something specific and ordinary, and taking the ten seconds required to write it down before it's forgotten.

## What it involves

- One short entry a day, usually a sentence or two
- No editing, no curation — the point is the habit, not the output
- Occasional collections, when a run of entries turns out to say something together

## Status

A daily practice, kept quietly and consistently.
