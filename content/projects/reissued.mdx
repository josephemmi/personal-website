Reissued is a small, ongoing project cataloguing vinyl reissues worth caring about — records that deserve better than being forgotten, and pressings that deserve better than being done badly.

It started as a personal list, kept for myself, of which reissue of a given album was actually worth buying. It grew into something closer to a proper archive: pressing notes, mastering credits, and short write-ups on why a particular record matters.

The project is equal parts curiosity and craftsmanship — a way of paying close attention to something I care about, and documenting it properly instead of letting the knowledge live only in scattered notes and half-remembered forum threads.

## What it involves

- Researching pressing histories and mastering credits for reissued albums
- Writing short, opinionated notes on which editions are worth seeking out
- Building a lightweight personal cataloguing system to track it all

## Status

Actively maintained, updated as new reissues catch my attention.
