AI Experiments is a running log of small, hands-on projects exploring what large language models and related tools are actually good for, as distinct from what they're marketed as being good for.

Each experiment is deliberately small and time-boxed — a weekend prototype, a scripted workflow, a tool built to solve one specific, real problem. The goal isn't to keep up with every new model release; it's to build enough hands-on intuition to have an informed opinion, as both a product person and a builder.

## Recent threads

- Prototyping small internal tools using AI-assisted coding workflows
- Testing where AI genuinely accelerates product discovery versus where it just produces plausible-sounding noise
- Building lightweight automations for personal projects like this website

## Status

Ongoing, in small, deliberately scoped bursts.
