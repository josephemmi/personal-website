Recipe app

Speak or type a recipe, get back an ingredient list and a shopping list.

Built with Claude Code & Cursor

Prototype

What it does

Tell it a recipe — by voice or by typing — and it pulls out the ingredients, then turns that into a shopping list. The idea was to remove the annoying middle step of manually transcribing a recipe into something you can actually shop from.

Where it came from

This was the first real thing built with Claude Code and Cursor, early in 2026 — less a product and more a way of finding out what these tools could actually do at the time. It was never finished. Development stopped once the initial experiment had answered the question it was built to answer.
