Encounter Echo

A lightweight tool for remembering people you meet — name, where, and a note, so the context doesn't disappear.

Built with Base44

Prototype

What it does

A single-purpose contact-memory app. Every entry is a person, with three fields: their name, where you met them, and a note — how you met, what you talked about, anything worth remembering next time. No CRM ambitions, just a way of not losing the thread on a good conversation.

Why it exists

One of the first real tests of what Base44's AI-driven builder could do — how far a no-code-adjacent tool could take a simple, well-defined idea without hand-written code.

Still missing. Real entries exist in the app, but a privacy pass is needed before anything from it is shown publicly — some of the data in there is genuinely personal and shouldn't be quoted, even as an example.
