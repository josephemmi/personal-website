Cue

A single queue for everything worth going back to — podcasts, videos, articles, all in one place.

Built with Base44 & Figma

Prototype

What it does

One queue instead of five. Save a podcast episode, a YouTube video, a TikTok, an article — anything with a URL — and track it through queued, playing, and done, with tags and light analytics on where your time actually goes: top sources, top shows, total time consumed.

Where it came from

The idea is nearly two years old — sketched out on paper before the AI tooling existed to build it properly. There's a Figma concept alongside the sketches. The Base44 build is further along than the original framing suggested: the data model and core logic are real and working, even if the content in it right now is mostly seed data rather than genuine daily use.

Still missing. The Figma visuals haven't been pulled in yet — once they are, this becomes a proper concept-to-prototype write-up with the sketches alongside the working app.
